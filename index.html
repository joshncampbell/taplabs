Questions people ask

Straight answers, not fine print.

Do your apps track me?
It depends on the app. Nine Bar and USVI Life are local‑first with no ad networks or analytics — your data stays on your device unless you choose to sync it. Coast Clear's free tier shows ads through Google AdSense, removable with a small yearly subscription. None of our apps sell your data.
Nine Bar, for example, works fully offline with no account required.
Is taplabs really just one person?
For now, yes. Every app is designed, built, and supported by one person — which is also why each one stays small and focused instead of trying to do everything.
Small team, small scope, on purpose.
Which platforms do you support?
iOS first. Coast Clear, Nine Bar, and USVI Life are all built natively in Swift — other platforms may follow once an app has found its footing.
No cross‑platform shortcuts, at least not yet.
How do you decide what to build next?
Usually a real, specific annoyance — tracking espresso shots in the Notes app, wondering if it's safe to swim. If a small app can fix it well, it's worth building.
Got an idea? Say hello at the bottom of the page.
