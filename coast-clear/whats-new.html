Beaches

Save the spots you actually visit.

Follow the beaches you care about and find them all in one place, with a status dot showing recent activity at a glance.

  • A new Following tab shows every beach you've followed, each with a color-coded status dot.
  • Follow or unfollow from any Beach Page or the All Beaches list — search, and sort by nearest or A–Z, make it easy to find yours.
Beach Pages

Every beach, its own page.

See what's actually been reported at a specific beach, without digging through the full map.

  • See the most recent reported sighting, how many reports came in over the past 24 hours, and a rolling list of recent activity.
  • A colored status icon — calm, caution, or alert — appears right at the top, plus a direct link that opens the map centered on that beach.
Widgets

Check in without opening the app.

See a followed beach's status right from your Home Screen — no need to launch Coast Clear first.

  • The small widget shows one followed beach; the medium widget shows up to three, each with its own status.
  • Widgets pick up automatically as reports change — tap one to jump straight into that beach's page.
