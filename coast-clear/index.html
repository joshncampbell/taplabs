Questions

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers for usage and expectations.

Are sightings on Coast Clear verified?

No. All sightings are user-reported and not verified by Coast Clear. Ocean conditions can shift in minutes, so always scan the water personally and follow local signage and lifeguard guidance.

Do I need an account to use it?

No traditional account is required — Coast Clear uses anonymous access so you can report sightings and follow beaches right away.

How much does it cost?

Coast Clear is free to use. The free tier includes ads; a subscription removes them.

Does Coast Clear sell my data?

No. Coast Clear doesn't sell your personal data. The free tier shows ads through Google's ad network, which a subscription removes.
