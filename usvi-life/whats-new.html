Widgets

Your saved reads, one glance away.

Add a Saved Articles widget to your Home Screen, and quickly jump back into articles you've saved.

  • Choose your size: small, medium, or large — the more room you give it, the more saved articles it shows.
  • Tap any article in the widget to jump straight back into it, no need to open the app and search first.
